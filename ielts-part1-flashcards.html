Quick tips to hit band 8 in Part 1
  • Answer, then extend — say your answer, then a quick because / but / so.
  • It's fine to be negative — a confident "not really, to be honest…" with a reason scores well.
  • Use contractions and fillers — "I'm", "it's", "I'd say", "to be honest", "actually" — natural, and they buy thinking time.
  • Don't memorise word-for-word — learn the pattern and the vocabulary, not the script.
