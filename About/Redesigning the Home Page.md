# Redesigning the homepage

## Intro

One of the most impactful projects I led was redesigning the Home Page of JD's merchant analytics platform, transforming it from ==a reporting dashboard== into ==a decision-support experience==.

At the time, the platform already ==provided comprehensive business data==, but ==merchant satisfaction had remained low== for several consecutive quarters. The common feedback was that merchants had plenty of data, but they ==struggled to==  identify  business problems and decide what actions to take next. Many stakeholders believed we should build AI-generated operational recommendations, but I wasn't convinced that was the biggest opportunity.

Before deciding on a solution, I ==interviewed== merchants of different sizes and ==analyzed== user behavior data to understand where they were getting stuck. One insight stood out: merchants didn't lack data--they ==struggled to== ~~identify the right problems~~ spot problems and act. Less experienced merchants often stopped on the Home Page, because they didn't know where to investigate next, while experienced merchants ~~naturally drilled down into detailed reports~~ could, but ~~even they~~ were limited by how few stores or products they could analyze at once.

Based on that insight, I shifted our product strategy. Instead of focusing on AI-generated recommendations, we prioritized helping merchants identify problems and locate root causes more efficiently. We redesign the Home Page around merchant's decision-making process, surfacing the business bottleneck, highlighting products and traffic channel responsible, guiding users into root-cause analysis before presenting operational recommendations.

After launch, merchants responded very positively, because they could identify problems more effectively and spent less time navigating through multiple reports. But more importantly, this project change how I think about product management. I learned that the best data products don't simple present more information, but reduce the efforts required for users to make better decisions.

我印象最深、成长最快的一个项目，是我主导的京东商家数据分析平台首页改版，把它从一个单纯的报表看板升级为一个决策支持型的产品体验。

在项目开始时，平台已经提供了非常丰富的经营数据，但商家满意度连续几个季度都比较低。核心反馈是：商家虽然能看到很多指标，但很难从中识别真正的问题，也不知道下一步该采取什么经营动作。团队内部当时一个比较主流的方向是做 AI 经营建议，希望自动生成运营策略，但我并不认为这是最大的问题。

在做方案决策前，我分别通过商家访谈和行为数据分析去理解用户真正卡住的地方。我发现一个非常关键的现象：商家并不是缺数据，而是缺“问题识别”的能力。尤其是低阶商家，往往停留在首页就结束分析，因为他们不知道下一步该看什么；而成熟商家则会自然地继续深入到明细和商品层级。

基于这个洞察，我调整了产品策略：不再优先做 AI 推荐，而是先解决“如何更快识别问题”的能力，把产品重点放在诊断和归因上。

因此我们重新设计了首页，把原来分散的指标，重构为围绕经营决策的问题链路：业务整体表现如何、收入损失发生在哪里、转化漏斗哪个环节异常、具体是哪些商品或流量渠道导致的。并进一步支持用户下钻到根因分析，再结合经营动作建议。

上线之后，商家反馈可以更快定位经营问题，分析路径明显缩短，减少了在多个报表之间跳转的成本。更重要的是，这个项目也改变了我对产品的理解：好的数据产品不只是提供更多信息，而是帮助用户更低成本地做出更好的决策。
