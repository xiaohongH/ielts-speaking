**Paragraph 1 — What cork is: a remarkable, ancient material**

Cork - the thick ==bark== of the cork oak tree (*Quercus suber*) - is ==a remarkable material==. It is ==tough==, ==elastic==, ==buoyant==, and ==fire-resistant==, and ==suitable for a wide range of purposes==. It has also been ==used for millennia==: the ancient Egyptians ==sealed their sarcophagi== (stone coffins) with cork, while the ancient Greeks and Romans used it for anything ==from beehives to sandals==.

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Paragraph 1
> 软木——即栓皮栎(学名 *Quercus suber*)那层厚厚的树皮——是一种非凡的材料。它坚韧、有弹性、能浮于水面、还耐火,适用于各种各样的用途。它的使用也已有数千年之久:古埃及人用软木密封他们的石棺,而古希腊人和古罗马人则把它用于从蜂箱到凉鞋的各种物件。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Paragraph 1
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **bark** (n.) = the outer covering of a tree 树皮。→ *the thick bark of the cork oak* 栓皮栎那层厚树皮。
> - **a remarkable material** = 一种非凡/了不起的材料。(*remarkable* = worth noticing 值得注意的)
> - **tough** (adj.) = strong, not easily damaged 坚韧的、结实的。
> - **elastic** (adj.) = able to return to its shape after being stretched/squashed 有弹性的。
> - **buoyant** (adj.) = able to float 能漂浮的、有浮力的。⭐ 高频词。
> - **fire-resistant** (adj.) = able to withstand fire 耐火的、抗火的。
> - **suitable for a wide range of purposes** = 适用于各种各样的用途。⭐ 好用的写作短语。
> - **used for millennia** = 使用了数千年。(*millennia* = *millennium* 的复数,一千年)
> - **sealed their sarcophagi** = 密封他们的石棺。(*seal* v. 密封;*sarcophagus* n. 石棺,复数 *sarcophagi*)
> - **from beehives to sandals** = 从蜂箱到凉鞋(用 "from A to B" 表示用途之广)。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** Cork is an extraordinary, all-purpose natural material — tough, elastic, buoyant and fire-resistant — that humans have used for thousands of years. 软木是一种非凡的、用途广泛的天然材料——坚韧、有弹性、能浮、耐火——人类已使用了数千年。

**Paragraph 2 — Why the cork oak is extraordinary: its cellular structure**

And the cork oak itself is ==an extraordinary tree==. Its bark grows up to 20 cm in thickness, ==insulating the tree like a coat== wrapped around the trunk and branches and keeping the inside at ==a constant 20 °C all year round==. ==Developed most probably as a defence against forest fires==, the bark of the cork oak has ==a particular cellular structure== - with about 40 million cells per cubic centimetre - that ==technology has never succeeded in replicating==. The cells are ==filled with air==, which is why cork is so buoyant. It also has ==an elasticity== that means you can ==squash== it and watch it ==spring back to its original size and shape== when you ==release the pressure==.

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Paragraph 2
> 而栓皮栎本身也是一种非凡的树。它的树皮厚度可达 20 厘米,像一件外套一样包裹着树干和树枝,为树木保温,使树的内部一年四季都保持恒定的 20 摄氏度。栓皮栎的树皮很可能是作为抵御森林火灾的一种防御机制而演化出来的,它拥有一种特殊的细胞结构——每立方厘米约有 4000 万个细胞——这是技术至今都未能成功复制的。这些细胞里充满了空气,这正是软木浮力如此之强的原因。它还具有一种弹性:你可以把它挤压变形,当你松开压力时,又能看着它回弹到原来的大小和形状。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Paragraph 2
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **an extraordinary tree** = 一种非凡的树。
> - **insulate** (v.) = to protect with a material that stops heat escaping 使隔热、保温、绝缘。→ *insulating the tree like a coat* 像外套一样为树保温。⭐ 名词 *insulation* 见 Para 5。
> - **a constant 20 °C all year round** = 一年四季恒定 20 摄氏度。(*constant* = 恒定的;*all year round* = 全年)
> - **developed … as a defence against forest fires** = (很可能)作为抵御森林火灾的防御机制而演化出来。(*a defence against* = 对……的防御)
> - **a particular cellular structure** = 一种特殊的细胞结构。(*cellular* = 细胞的)
> - **cells per cubic centimetre** = 每立方厘米的细胞数。
> - **technology has never succeeded in replicating** = 技术从未成功复制(过)。(*replicate* = 复制、仿制)⭐ 强调其独特、难以人工再造。
> - **filled with air** = 充满空气。→ 这解释了为什么 buoyant。
> - **elasticity** (n.) = 弹性。(形容词 *elastic* 见 Para 1)
> - **squash** (v.) = to press/crush into a flatter shape 挤压、压扁。
> - **spring back to its original size and shape** = 回弹到原来的大小和形状。(*spring back* = 弹回)
> - **release the pressure** = 释放/松开压力。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** The cork oak's bark is a natural insulator with a unique air-filled cellular structure (likely evolved against fire) that no technology can copy — this explains cork's buoyancy and elasticity. 栓皮栎的树皮是天然的隔热层,拥有独特的、充满空气的细胞结构(很可能是为抵御火灾而演化),这是任何技术都无法复制的——这解释了软木的浮力与弹性。

**Paragraph 3 — Where cork oaks grow: the Mediterranean and Portugal**

Cork oaks grow in a number of ==Mediterranean== countries, including Portugal, Spain, Italy, Greece and Morocco. They ==flourish in warm, sunny climates== where there is ==a minimum of== 400 millimetres of rain per year, and ==not more than== 800 millimetres. Like grape vines, the trees ==thrive in poor soil==, ==putting down deep roots== in search of ==moisture and nutrients==. Southern Portugal's Alentejo region ==meets all of these requirements==, which explains why, by the early 20th century, this region had become the world's ==largest producer of cork==, and why today it ==accounts for roughly half of all cork production== around the world.

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Paragraph 3
> 栓皮栎生长在一些地中海国家,包括葡萄牙、西班牙、意大利、希腊和摩洛哥。它们在温暖、阳光充足的气候中茁壮生长,那里每年至少有 400 毫米、但不超过 800 毫米的降雨。与葡萄藤一样,这种树能在贫瘠的土壤中生长旺盛,向下扎下深根以寻找水分和养分。葡萄牙南部的阿连特茹(Alentejo)地区满足所有这些条件,这就解释了为什么到 20 世纪初,该地区已成为世界上最大的软木产地,以及为什么如今它约占全球软木总产量的一半。

> **Main idea 主旨:** Cork oaks need a specific warm, dry-ish Mediterranean climate and poor soil; Portugal's Alentejo region fits perfectly and produces about half the world's cork. 栓皮栎需要特定的温暖、少雨的地中海气候和贫瘠土壤;葡萄牙的阿连特茹地区条件完美,产出约占全球一半的软木。

**Paragraph 4 — Cork production: an exercise in patience**

Most cork forests are ==family-owned==. Many of these family businesses, and indeed many of the trees themselves, are around 200 years old. Cork production is, above all, ==an exercise in patience==. From the planting of a cork ==sapling== to the first ==harvest== takes 25 years, and ==a gap of approximately a decade must separate harvests== from an individual tree. And for ==top-quality cork==, it's necessary to wait a further 15 or 20 years. You even have to wait for the right kind of summer's day to harvest cork. If the bark is ==stripped== on a day when it's too cold - or when the air is ==damp== - the tree will be ==damaged==.

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Paragraph 4
> 大多数软木林都是家族所有。许多这样的家族企业,乃至许多树木本身,都有约 200 年的历史。软木生产首先是一种对耐心的考验。从栽下一棵软木树苗到第一次采收需要 25 年,而同一棵树的两次采收之间必须间隔大约十年。若想获得顶级软木,还需再等 15 到 20 年。你甚至得等到合适的夏日才能采收软木。如果在过冷的日子——或空气潮湿时——剥取树皮,树就会受损。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Paragraph 4
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **family-owned** (adj.) = 家族所有的、家族经营的。
> - **an exercise in patience** = 一件需要极大耐心的事。⭐ *an exercise in X* = 一次对 X 的考验/实践(如 *an exercise in futility* 徒劳之举)。
> - **above all** = most importantly 最重要的是、首先。
> - **sapling** (n.) = a young tree 树苗、幼树。
> - **harvest** (n./v.) = 收获、采收。→ *the first harvest* 第一次采收。
> - **a gap of approximately a decade must separate harvests** = 两次采收之间必须间隔大约十年。(*a decade* = 十年;*separate* 在此=间隔)
> - **top-quality cork** = 顶级(最高品质)软木。
> - **strip** (v.) = to pull/remove a covering 剥去、剥离。→ *the bark is stripped* 树皮被剥取。⭐ 见 Para 5 *stripping / cork-strippers*。
> - **damp** (adj.) = slightly wet 潮湿的。
> - **damaged** (adj./v.) = 受损的、损坏。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** Cork farming demands extreme patience: 25 years to the first harvest, ~10-year gaps between harvests, extra decades for top quality, and even the right weather — otherwise the tree is harmed. 软木种植需要极大耐心:第一次采收要 25 年,两次采收间隔约 10 年,顶级品质还要再等数十年,甚至要挑对天气——否则会伤到树。

**Paragraph 5 — Harvesting: a specialised, manual profession, and cork's uses**

Cork harvesting is a very ==specialised profession==. ==No mechanical means of stripping cork bark has been invented==, so the job is done by teams of ==highly skilled workers==. First, they make ==vertical cuts== down the bark using small ==sharp axes==, then ==lever it away== in pieces as large as they can manage. The most skilful cork-strippers ==prise away== a ==semi-circular husk== that ==runs the length of the trunk== from just above ground level to the first branches. It is then ==dried on the ground== for about four months, before being taken to factories, where it is ==boiled to kill any insects== that might remain in the cork. Over 60% of cork then goes on to be made into traditional ==bottle stoppers==, with most of ==the remainder== being used in the ==construction trade==. Corkboard and cork tiles are ideal for ==thermal and acoustic insulation==, while ==granules of cork== are used in the manufacture of concrete.

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Paragraph 5
> 软木采收是一项非常专业的职业。目前尚未发明剥取软木树皮的机械手段,因此这项工作由一队队技术高超的工人来完成。首先,他们用锋利的小斧在树皮上纵向切割,然后尽可能地把大块树皮撬离下来。技艺最娴熟的剥皮工会撬下一整块半圆形的"外壳",它沿着树干从略高于地面处一直延伸到第一根树枝。之后,树皮会在地上晾晒约四个月,再被运往工厂,在那里煮沸以杀死可能残留在软木中的昆虫。随后,超过 60% 的软木被制成传统的瓶塞,其余大部分则用于建筑行业。软木板和软木砖是理想的隔热与隔音材料,而软木颗粒则被用于混凝土的制造。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Paragraph 5
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **specialised profession** = 专业化的职业。
> - **no mechanical means … has been invented** = 尚未发明出机械手段。(*mechanical means* = 机械方法) ⭐ 强调纯手工。
> - **highly skilled workers** = 技术高超的工人。
> - **vertical cuts** = 纵向/垂直的切口。
> - **sharp axes** = 锋利的斧子。(*axe* n. 斧,复数 *axes*)
> - **lever … away** = to pull away using a lever action 用杠杆之力撬开、撬离。
> - **prise away** (v.) = to force away with effort 撬开、撬下。(≈ pry away;与 lever away 同义复现)
> - **a semi-circular husk** = 一块半圆形的"外壳"/皮壳。(*semi-circular* 半圆形的;*husk* 外壳)
> - **runs the length of the trunk** = 沿树干全长延伸。(*run the length of* = 贯穿……的全长)
> - **dried on the ground** = 在地上晾干。
> - **boiled to kill any insects** = 煮沸以杀死昆虫。
> - **bottle stoppers** = 瓶塞。⭐ 全文关键词(下文讨论其被替代)。
> - **the remainder** (n.) = the part that is left 其余部分、剩余。
> - **construction trade** = 建筑行业。
> - **thermal and acoustic insulation** = 隔热与隔音(隔声)。⭐ *thermal* 热的;*acoustic* 声音的;*insulation* 隔绝(材料)。
> - **granules of cork** = 软木颗粒。(*granule* = 小颗粒)
>
> **⚠️ OCR note:** the source reads *"than lever it away"* — a typo for *"**then** lever it away"* 然后再撬离。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** Harvesting is skilled, entirely manual work (no machine exists); the bark is cut, prised off, dried and boiled. Most cork becomes bottle stoppers, the rest goes to construction (insulation, concrete). 采收是纯手工的技术活(无机器可用);树皮经切割、撬取、晾晒、煮沸处理。多数软木做成瓶塞,其余用于建筑(隔热隔音、混凝土)。

**Paragraph 6 — The end of cork's monopoly: the TCA problem and substitutes**

Recent years have seen ==the end of the virtual monopoly== of cork as the material for bottle stoppers, due to ==concerns about the effect it may have on the contents of the bottle==. This is caused by ==a chemical compound== called 2,4,6-trichloroanisole (**TCA**), which forms through ==the interaction of== plant ==phenols==, ==chlorine== and ==mould==. ==The tiniest concentrations== - as little as three or four ==parts per trillion== - can ==spoil the taste== of the product contained in the bottle. The result has been ==a gradual yet steady move== first towards ==plastic stoppers== and, more recently, to ==aluminium screw caps==. These ==substitutes== are ==cheaper to manufacture== and, in the case of screw caps, ==more convenient for the user==.

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Paragraph 6
> 近年来,软木作为瓶塞材料的近乎垄断的地位已宣告终结,原因是人们担心它可能对瓶中物产生影响。这是由一种名为 2,4,6-三氯苯甲醚(TCA)的化合物引起的,该物质通过植物酚、氯和霉菌的相互作用而形成。极其微小的浓度——低至万亿分之三或四——就足以破坏瓶中产品的味道。其结果是,人们逐渐但稳步地先转向塑料瓶塞,更近来又转向铝制螺旋盖。这些替代品制造成本更低,而就螺旋盖而言,对使用者也更方便。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Paragraph 6
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **the end of the virtual monopoly** = 近乎垄断地位的终结。(*virtual* = 几乎的、实际上的;*monopoly* = 垄断)⭐ 高频。
> - **concerns about the effect it may have on …** = 对它可能造成的影响的担忧。⭐ 万能句型 *concerns about the effect X may have on Y*。
> - **the contents of the bottle** = 瓶中物、瓶内容物。
> - **a chemical compound** = 一种化合物。
> - **the interaction of A, B and C** = A、B、C 三者的相互作用。
> - **phenols / chlorine / mould** = 酚类 / 氯 / 霉菌。(*mould* 英式拼写 = *mold*)
> - **the tiniest concentrations** = 极其微小的浓度。(*concentration* = 浓度)
> - **parts per trillion** = 万亿分之(几)。⭐ 表示极低浓度。
> - **spoil the taste** = 破坏(毁掉)味道。(*spoil* = 破坏、使变质)
> - **a gradual yet steady move (towards)** = 逐渐但稳步地转向。(*gradual yet steady* 循序而稳定)
> - **plastic stoppers / aluminium screw caps** = 塑料瓶塞 / 铝制螺旋盖。
> - **substitutes** (n.) = replacements 替代品。⭐ 高频。
> - **cheaper to manufacture** = 制造成本更低。
> - **more convenient for the user** = 对用户更方便。
>
> **⚠️ OCR note:** the source reads *"three or four parts **o a** trillion"* — a typo. Correct: *"three or four parts **per** trillion"* 万亿分之三或四。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** Cork's near-monopoly on bottle stoppers has ended because a compound (TCA) can taint a bottle's contents even in tiny amounts, pushing producers towards cheaper, more convenient plastic stoppers and aluminium screw caps. 软木在瓶塞上的近乎垄断已终结:因为一种化合物(TCA)即便极微量也能污染瓶中物,促使人们转向更便宜、更方便的塑料塞与铝制螺旋盖。

**Paragraph 7 — Cork's advantages: image, sustainability and the environment**

The classic cork stopper does have ==several advantages==, however. Firstly, its ==traditional image== is more ==in keeping with== that of the type of ==high quality goods== with which it has long been ==associated==. Secondly - and very importantly - cork is ==a sustainable product== that can be ==recycled without difficulty==. Moreover, cork forests are a resource which ==support local biodiversity==, and ==prevent desertification== in the regions where they are planted. So, ==given the current concerns about environmental issues==, the future of this ancient material ==once again looks promising==.

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Paragraph 7
> 然而,经典的软木瓶塞确实有几大优势。首先,它那传统的形象更契合它长期以来所关联的那类高品质商品的形象。其次——也非常重要的一点——软木是一种可持续的产品,可以毫不费力地回收利用。此外,软木林是一种能支持当地生物多样性、并在其种植地区防止荒漠化的资源。因此,鉴于当前人们对环境问题的关注,这种古老材料的前景再次显得光明。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Paragraph 7

## 🧠 Whole-passage summary 全文总结

**EN:** Cork — the thick bark of the cork oak — is a **remarkable material**: tough, elastic, buoyant and fire-resistant, and used since ancient times. The tree is **extraordinary** too: its bark has a unique air-filled **cellular structure** (probably evolved as a **defence against fire**) that no technology can replicate, giving cork its buoyancy and elasticity. Cork oaks need a specific **Mediterranean** climate and poor soil, so Portugal's **Alentejo** produces about half the world's cork. Production is **an exercise in patience** (25 years to first harvest, ~10-year gaps) and harvesting is skilled, entirely **manual** work; most cork becomes **bottle stoppers**, the rest goes to **construction** (insulation, concrete). Recently a compound (**TCA**) that taints wine even in **parts per trillion** ended cork's near-**monopoly**, pushing producers towards cheaper **plastic stoppers** and **aluminium screw caps**. Still, cork keeps real advantages — a premium **traditional image**, easy **recyclability**, and forests that support **biodiversity** and prevent **desertification** — so its future "once again looks promising."

**中文:** 软木——栓皮栎的厚树皮——是一种**非凡的材料**:坚韧、有弹性、能浮、耐火,自古沿用。这种树本身也很**奇特**:其树皮拥有独特的、充满空气的**细胞结构**(很可能是为**抵御火灾**而演化),任何技术都无法复制,这赋予了软木浮力与弹性。栓皮栎需要特定的**地中海**气候和贫瘠土壤,因此葡萄牙的**阿连特茹**产出约占全球一半的软木。生产是**耐心的考验**(首采要 25 年,采收间隔约 10 年),采收是纯**手工**的技术活;多数软木做成**瓶塞**,其余用于**建筑**(隔热隔音、混凝土)。近来,一种即便**万亿分之几**也能污染酒味的化合物(**TCA**)终结了软木近乎**垄断**的地位,促使人们转向更便宜的**塑料塞**与**铝制螺旋盖**。不过,软木仍保有实在优势——高端的**传统形象**、易于**回收**,以及能支持**生物多样性**、防止**荒漠化**的软木林——因此其前景"再次显得光明"。
