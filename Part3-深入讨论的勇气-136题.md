## 技能

### What skills are important to success in the knowledge-based economy?
（"是什么"类考题）
在知识经济时代，获得成功的重要技能有哪些？

**参考答案：**
**Independent thinking skills** （独立思考的技能） and **problem-solving skills** （解决问题的技能） are very important to success in the knowledge-based economy. They help people **develop new ideas** on their own. For example, employees who have good independent thinking skills and problem-solving skills can often **find practical and creative solutions** （找到实用而且有创意的解决办法） to their tasks. Communication skills （沟通技能） and **teamwork skills** （团队合作技能） are also very important to success in the knowledge-based economy because they help people to **work closely together** （一起紧密地合作） and **achieve their common goal** （实现他们的共同目标）.

**词汇：**
- **independent thinking skills** — 独立思考的技能
- **problem-solving skills** — 解决问题的技能
- **develop new ideas** — 形成新的想法
- **find practical and creative solutions** — 找到实用而且有创意的解决办法
- **communication skills** — 沟通技能
- **teamwork skills** — 团队合作技能
- **work closely together** — 一起紧密地合作
- **achieve their common goal** — 实现他们的共同目标
- 更多 ideas：
  - **computer skills** — 计算机技能
  - **leadership skills** — 领导才能（注意：地道英文里不说 leader skills ✗）

### Why do some people choose to do new things instead of the same old things?
（原因类考题）
为什么有些人选择从事新的事情而不是重复一直在做的事情？
※ 答题时可以参考 British Council 推荐的 3 种结构 （Day 7），同时也适当给自己一些灵活度，考官希望听到的是自然、真实的回答，而不是"套路"

**参考答案：**
They choose to do new things because they find it more **interesting and motivating** than doing the same old things. Doing new things can help them **gain new knowledge**, **develop new skills** and **make new friends**. For example, some people choose to change jobs so that they can develop new skills and **have new experiences**.

**词汇：**
- **interesting and motivating** — 有趣而且给人动力的
- **gain new knowledge** — 获取新的知识
- **develop new skills** — 发展新的技能
- **make new friends** — 结识新的朋友
- **so that ...** — 这样就可以……，以便……
- **have new experiences** — 有新的经历

### Do you think schools should teach children how to manage money?
（"你怎么看"类考题）
你是否认为学校应该教儿童如何管理财务？

**参考答案：**
Yes, I think they should because financial education can help children **develop their financial skills** （发展他们的财务技能）. For instance, they can learn how to **make a budget** （确定预算） and **save money for the future**. Financial education in schools can also help children **develop good spending habits** （形成良好的消费习惯）.

**词汇：**
- **develop their financial skills** — 发展他们的财务技能
- **make a budget** — 确定预算
- **save money for the future** — 为将来省钱
- **develop good spending habits** — 形成良好的消费习惯

### Do you think children should learn to make things by hand?
（"你怎么看"类考题）
你是否认为儿童应该学习手工制作物品？

**参考答案：**
Yes, I suppose they should. Learning to make things by hand can **make children more creative** （让他们变得更有创意）. For example, learning to **make pottery** （制作陶艺） can **encourage imagination and creativity** （激发想象力和创造力） in children. Also, learning to make things by hand can **improve children's concentration** （增强他们的注意力） and **hand-eye coordination** （手眼协调能力）.

**词汇：**
- **make them more creative** — 让他们变得更有创意
- **make pottery** — 制作陶艺
- **encourage imagination and creativity** — 激发想象力和创造力
- **improve children's concentration and hand-eye coordination** — 增强儿童的注意力和手眼协调能力

儿童可以在学校里学到哪些技能？

**参考答案：**

- **computer skills** — 计算机技能

### What skills can children learn at home but not at school?
（"是什么"类考题）
儿童可以在家里学到哪些在学校里学不到的技能？

**参考答案：**
They can learn a variety of **life skills** （生活技能） at home, which they can't learn at school. For example, their parents can teach them **cooking skills** and **home-decorating skills**. They can also learn how to **repair things** at home.

**词汇：**
- **life skills** — 生活技能，注意：native speakers 不说 living skills ✗
- **cooking skills** — 做饭的技能
- **home-decorating skills** — 家庭装饰的技能
- **repair things** — 维修物品

## 学习

### Do you think children should be encouraged to think independently?
（"你怎么看"类考题）
你是否认为儿童应该被鼓励独立思考？

**参考答案：**
Definitely. When they become adults, they will not always have people to **guide them** （指导他们）. For example, they will need to decide where they want to live, what careers they want to have and what kind of lifestyle they want to have. **If we don't encourage children to think independently, they will rely on others' ideas** （依赖别人的想法） and they will be less successful when they become adults.

**词汇：**
- **independent thinking skills** — 独立思考的技能
- **guide them** — 指导他们
- **rely on others' ideas** — 依赖于别人的想法
- **If we don't ..., ... will ...** — 如果我们不……，……将会……（在答案结尾常用的对相反情况进行假设的句式）

### What are the differences between the way children learn and the way adults learn?
（对比类考题）
儿童的学习方式和成年人的学习方式区别是什么？

**参考答案：**
Children are good at remembering information because they like to **copy what they see and hear** （模仿他们看到和听到的内容）. For example, primary school pupils are generally better at remembering **historical dates** （历史事件的日期） and learning the **pronunciation** （发音） of new words than adult learners. By contrast, adult learners tend to **learn through understanding** （通过理解来学习）. They try to understand new information before trying to memorise it.

**词汇：**
- **copy what they see and hear** — 模仿他们看到和听到的内容
- **historical dates** — 历史事件的日期
- **pronunciation** — 发音
- **By contrast, ...** — 与此形成对比，……
- **learn through understanding** — 通过理解来学习
- **memorise** — 记忆
- 更多 ideas：
  - 儿童的学习特点还有：like to learn through interactive activities （喜欢通过互动的活动来学习）
  - 成年学习者的特点还有：are more likely to **think independently** （更有可能独立地思考） 和 are more likely to **think critically** （更有可能辩证地思考）

### Which are more important, academic lessons or art lessons?
（对比类考题）
学术课程更重要还是艺术课程更重要？

**参考答案：**
I would say academic lessons are more important because they help students develop a variety of **key skills** （关键技能）, such as **reading and writing skills**, **maths skills**, **foreign language skills** and **computer skills**. These skills can help students **prepare for employment** （为就业做准备）. Art lessons are **interesting and enjoyable** （有趣而且令人愉快的）, but most students won't need drawing or painting skills when they **enter the job market** （进入就业市场）.

**词汇：**
- **key skills / essential skills** — 关键技能
- **reading and writing skills** — 读写技能
- **maths skills** — 数学技能
- **foreign language skills** — 外语技能
- **computer skills** — 计算机技能
- **prepare for employment** — 为就业做准备
- **interesting and enjoyable** — 有趣而且令人愉快的
- **enter the job market** — 进入就业市场

### What are the qualities that good teachers have?
（"是什么"类考题）
好老师具有哪些素质？

**参考答案：**
Good teachers **know their subject well** （精通他们所教的科目）. They are also very **patient** （耐心的） and **enjoy sharing their knowledge with** their students. Another quality that good teachers have is **a good sense of humour** （幽默感）. They **make learning fun** （让学习变得有趣） and can keep their students **motivated to learn** （很有动力去学习）.

**词汇：**
- **know their subject well** — 精通他们所教的科目
- **patient** — 有耐心的
- **enjoy sharing their knowledge with their students** — 享受和他们的学生们分享知识
- **have a good sense of humour** — 很有幽默感
- **make learning fun** — 让学习变得有趣
- **motivated to learn** — 很有动力去学习
- 更多 ideas：
  - native speakers 讨论好老师的素质时还常说这句话：They encourage their students to **think independently** （鼓励学生们独立地思考）

### How has teaching changed in your country?
（对比类考题）
教学在你们国家有怎样的变化？

**参考答案：**
In the past, not many teachers used technology in the classroom. And there were very few classroom activities that **encouraged imagination** （激发想象力）. Nowadays, many teachers use technology to **improve their teaching** （改进他们的教学）. For example, these days, teachers often use **computers and projectors** （计算机和投影仪） to show their students pictures or videos, which makes their lectures more **interesting and enjoyable** （有趣而且令人愉快的）. There are also more **classroom activities** that **encourage imagination and creativity** （激发想象力和创造力） than there were in the past.

**词汇：**
- **improve their teaching** — 改进他们的教学
- **these days, ...** — 现在，……（native speakers 在口语里经常将 these days 和 nowadays 互换使用）
- **computers and projectors** — 计算机和投影仪
- **interesting and enjoyable** — 有趣而且令人愉快的
- **classroom activities** — 课堂活动
- **encourage imagination and creativity** — 激发想象力和创造力

### What are the advantages and disadvantages of giving prizes to children who do well at school?
（利弊类考题）
给在学校表现出色的儿童发奖的利与弊是什么？

**参考答案：**
The main benefit is prizes can **make children more motivated** （让儿童们更有动力） and help them **achieve their potential** （充分发挥出他们的潜力） at school. For example, children who win prizes in maths competitions at school **are likely to** study maths even harder in the future. The main drawback is some children may think the reason for attending school is to win prizes **instead of** （而不是） **gaining knowledge** （获取知识） and **developing useful skills** （发展有用的技能）. And children who don't win prizes may feel that they are **not treated fairly** （没有受到公正的对待）.

**词汇：**
- **benefit / advantage** — 好处，这两个词在回答利弊类问题时可以互换使用
- **drawback / disadvantage** — 弊端，它们在回答利弊类问题时也可以互换使用
- **make children more motivated** — 让儿童们更有动力
- **achieve their potential** — 充分发挥出他们的潜力，注意：potential （潜力） 是不可数名词，不能加 s
- **are likely to ...** — 很可能会做某事
- **instead of** — 而不是
- **gain knowledge** — 获取知识
- **develop useful skills** — 发展有用的技能
- **not treated fairly** — 受到不公正的对待

### Will the traditional classroom disappear in the future?
（展望未来类考题）
传统的教室将来是否会消失？

**参考答案：**
It will probably become less common, but I don't think it will disappear. I think students will spend more time **studying online** in the future. For example, many university lectures will be moved online so that students can **listen to the lectures anytime and anywhere**. On the other hand, students will still need to **attend lessons** in a classroom at least a few times a week to **interact face to face with** （与……面对面地交流） their teachers and classmates.

**词汇：**
- **attend lessons** — 上课
- **interact face to face with ...** — 与……面对面地交流

## 工作

### What kinds of jobs do young people like to do in your country?
（"是什么"类考题）
在你国家里年轻人喜欢从事哪些种类的工作？

**参考答案：**
IT jobs and finance jobs are popular among young people in my country. These jobs **offer high salaries** （提供高薪） and **the demand for them** （对它们的需求） is increasing fast. For example, IT engineers **earn high salaries** and the demand for them continues to grow in my country.

**词汇：**
- **IT jobs** — 信息技术工作
- **finance jobs** — 金融工作
- **offer high salaries** — 提供高薪
- **the demand for them** — 对它们的需求
- **earn high salaries** — 挣高薪

### What are the main things to consider when choosing a job?
（"是什么"类考题）
选择工作时主要考虑的因素有哪些？

**参考答案：**
For most people, the main things to consider when choosing a job are **the salary** （薪酬）, **the benefits** （工作福利） and whether the job **matches their interests** （符合他们的兴趣）. For example, most people consider it very important to earn a salary that allows them to live comfortably. Other things that many people also consider when choosing a job include **the working hours** （工作时间）, **the location of the workplace** （工作地点） and **training opportunities** （培训机会）.

**词汇：**
- **the salary** — 薪酬
- **the benefits** — 工作福利
- **matches their interests** — 符合他们的兴趣
- **the working hours** — 工作时间
- **the location of the workplace** — 工作地点
- **training opportunities** — 培训机会

### Which is better, staying in the same job for a long time or changing jobs every few years?
（对比类考题）
长期做同一工作更好还是每隔几年就换工作更好？

**参考答案：**
**长期做同一工作的好处：**
- **have more opportunities for promotion** — （长期做同一工作的人）有更多的升职机会
- **are more valued by their employers** — 更受雇主的重视
- **have stronger relationships with their colleagues** — 与同事们之间的关系更紧密
- **have more stability in their lives** — 有更多的生活稳定性

**每隔几年就换工作的好处：**
- **develop new skills** — 发展新的技能
- **make new friends** — 结识新的朋友
- **avoid falling into a routine** — 避免陷入重复的日常惯例当中
- **challenging and exciting** — 有挑战而且令人兴奋的

### What are the differences between older employees and young employees?
（对比类考题）
年长的员工和年轻的员工有哪些区别？

**参考答案：**
**年长的员工：**
- **more patient and detail-oriented** — 更耐心而且关注细节
- **more loyal to their employer** — 对雇主更忠诚
- **have more professional experience and life experience** — 有更多的专业经验和人生经验

**年轻的员工：**
- **more energetic and ambitious** — 更有活力和志向
- **have more creative ideas** — 有更多有创造力的想法
- **are more open-minded and willing to accept new ideas** — 思想更开放而且愿意接受新的想法

### Do you think women should be able to do all the same jobs that men do?
（"你怎么看"类考题）
你是否认为女性应该可以从事与男性相同的所有职业？

**参考答案：**
I think women should be able to do **most** of the jobs that men do. People should be employed **according to their abilities** （根据能力） rather than their **gender** （性别）. Therefore, women should **have equal job opportunities** （有平等的就业机会） as men in **the knowledge-based economy** （知识经济）. On the other hand, it's true that some jobs still **require great physical strength** （需要很强的体力）, such as **construction workers** （建筑工人） and firefighters （消防员）. These jobs tend to be **more challenging for women** than for men.

**词汇：**
- **according to their abilities** — 根据能力
- **gender** — 性别
- **have equal job opportunities** — 有平等的就业机会
- **the knowledge-based economy** — 知识经济
- **require great physical strength** — 需要很强的体力
- **construction workers** — 建筑工人
- **firefighters** — 消防员
- **more challenging** — 更有挑战的

### Do you think working from home will be more common in the future?
（展望未来类考题）
你是否认为在家远程上班将来会变得更常见？

**参考答案：**
Yes, I think it will be more common because the Internet will become **faster and more reliable** （更快而且更可靠） in the future, and there will be more **apps** that can help people **work efficiently** （更高效地工作） from home. Another reason is that I think in the future, there will be more people who **care about the environment** （关心环境）. As a result, more employers will allow their employees to work from home to help **reduce pollution from traffic**.

**词汇：**
- **faster and more reliable** — 更快而且更可靠
- **apps** — 应用程序
- **work efficiently** — 更高效地工作
- **care about the environment** — 关心环境
- **As a result, ...** — 因此，……
- **reduce pollution from traffic** — 减少交通污染

## 网络

### What are some problems that might be caused by using the Internet?
（利弊类考题）
使用互联网可能产生的问题有哪些？

**参考答案：**
The main problem is our **personal information** （个人信息） may be stolen when our mobile phones or computers are connected to the Internet. For example, some **hackers** （黑客） use **malware** （恶意软件） to steal personal information from mobile phones and computers. Another problem is people tend to **develop an inactive lifestyle** （形成缺少运动的生活方式） if they spend too much time on **social media** （社交媒体） or **online games** （网络游戏）.

**词汇：**
- **personal information** — 个人信息
- **hackers** — 黑客
- **develop an inactive lifestyle** — 形成缺少运动的生活方式
- **malware** — 恶意软件
- **social media** — 社交媒体
- **online games** — 网络游戏
- 更多 ideas：
  - 使用互联网可能产生的问题还有：
    - Some people **rely too much on the Internet**. 有些人过度地依赖网络。
    - It **reduces face-to-face interaction**. 互联网减少面对面的交流。
    - Many people prefer to **make friends in a virtual world**. 很多人更喜欢在一个虚拟的世界里交友。
    - They tend to **feel lonely and isolated in real life**. 他们往往在真实的生活里感到孤独而且孤立。
  - 使用互联网的好处则可以有：
    - The Internet is **an important source of information**. 互联网是重要的信息来源。

It helps us **find information quickly**. 它帮助我们快速地找到信息。

We can use social media to **keep in touch with family and friends**. 我们可以用社交媒体与亲友保持联系。

We can **listen to music**, **get news** and **share photos and videos** online. 我们可以在网上听音乐，获取新闻并且分享照片和视频。

### Which do you think are better, emails or hand-written letters?
（对比类考题）
你认为是电子邮件更好还是手写的书信更好？

**参考答案：**
It depends on the situation. Emails are usually better because they are **free** and they **arrive immediately** （立刻到达）. But I suppose hand-written letters are better when we want to **express love or appreciation** （表达爱或者感激）because hand-written letters feel **more personal** （更个人化）than emails. For example, a **hand-written thank-you letter** （手写的感谢信）is a better way to express appreciation than an email.

**词汇：**
- **arrive immediately** — 立刻到达

## 环境

### What can be done about global warming?
（方法类考题）
可以怎样应对全球变暖？

**参考答案：**
A variety of things can be done to improve the situation. Governments can take measures to **reduce carbon emissions** （减少碳排放）from **cars and factories**. For example, they can **put high taxes on petrol** （对汽油征收很高的税）, which will help to reduce the use of cars. They can also **give more financial support to** （提供更多的资助）research on **clean energy** （清洁能源）, such as solar energy and wind energy. As individuals （作为个人）, we can try to **walk or cycle to work or school** whenever possible. We can also **take public transport** more often.

**词汇：**
- **reduce carbon emissions** — 减少碳排放
- **cars and factories** — 汽车和工厂
- **put high taxes on petrol** — 对汽油征收很高的税
- **give more financial support to ...** — 为……提供更多的资助
- **clean energy** — 清洁能源，例如 solar energy （太阳能）、wind energy （风能）
- **as individuals** — 作为个人
- **walk or cycle to work or school** — 走路或者骑自行车上班或者上学
- **take public transport** — 乘坐公共交通

### What are the advantages and disadvantages of using clean energy, such as solar energy and wind energy?
（利弊类考题）

**参考答案：**
The main benefit is clean energy is **eco-friendly** （环保的）and **doesn't produce carbon emissions** （不产生碳排放）. Another benefit is clean energy is **renewable** （可再生的）. Therefore, new technology needs to be developed to make it **cheaper and more efficient** （更便宜而且更高效的）.

**词汇：**
- **eco-friendly** — 环保的
- **doesn't produce carbon emissions** — 不产生碳排放
- **renewable** — 可再生的
- **can't be used up** — 不会被用尽
- **fossil fuels** — 化石能源
- **cheaper and more efficient** — 更便宜而且更高效的

## 动植物

### What kind of influence do human activities have on wild animals?
（"是什么"类考题）
人类的活动对于野生动物有怎样的影响？

**参考答案：**
The **natural habitats** （自然栖息地）of many wild animals **are under threat** （受到威胁）from human activities. For example, **carbon emissions** （碳排放）from cars and factories **contribute to global warming** （加剧全球变暖）. As a result, the natural habitats of many wild animals, such as polar bears, are under threat. In some areas, the **construction** of new buildings and roads also causes many wild animals to **lose their natural habitats**.

**词汇：**
- **natural habitats** — 自然栖息地，注意：habitats （栖息地）和 habits （习惯）的发音不同
- **are under threat** — 受到威胁
- **carbon emissions** — 碳排放
- **contribute to global warming** — 加剧全球变暖（当 contribute to 的后面是负面事物时，它是"加剧"的意思，例如：contribute to climate change 加剧气候变化）
- **construction** — 建设

### How can we protect wild animals and plants?
（方法类考题）
我们可以怎样保护野生动植物？

**参考答案：**
Governments should **create more nature reserves** （创建更多的自然保护区）and **fine** companies that damage **natural habitats** （自然栖息地）. For instance, companies that cut down forests should **be heavily fined** （被重罚）. As individuals, we should **walk or cycle more and drive less** because many wild animals and plants **are under threat** from **global warming** （全球变暖）.

**词汇：**
- **governments** — 在英式英语里，government 的复数 governments 是泛指各级政府、各地政府或各国政府
- **create more nature reserves** — 创建更多的自然保护区，nature reserves 是"自然保护区"的意思
- [BBC例句] **Nature reserves** give them the opportunity to learn about local wild animals and plants.
- **natural habitats** — 野生动植物的自然栖息地
- **fine ...** — 罚款
- **walk or cycle more and drive less** — 英文的一句流行环保用语：更多地步行或者骑自行车，更少开车
- **are under threat** — 受到威胁
- **global warming** — 全球变暖

**更多 ideas：**
- 保护野生动植物的另一个重要措施是 Schools should educate students about **endangered animals and plants**. （学校应该帮助学生们了解濒危动植物。）
