## Stage 1 — Choosing an angle and scope 确定切入点与范围

**TRUDIE:** OK, Stewart. We need to start planning our paper on public libraries. Have you thought of ==an angle== yet?

**STEWART:** Well, there's so much we could look into. How libraries have ==changed over the centuries==, for instance, or how different countries organise them. What do you think, Trudie?

**TRUDIE:** Maybe we should concentrate on this country, and try and ==relate the changes in libraries to external developments==, like the fact that far more people can read than a century ago, and that the local population may speak lots of different languages.

**STEWART:** We could include something about changes in ==the source of funding==, too.

**TRUDIE:** Yes, but remember we're only supposed to write ==a short paper==, so it's probably best if we don't ==go into funding in any detail==.

**STEWART:** Right. Well, shall we just ==brainstorm== a few ideas, to get started?

> 🔑 an angle 切入点/角度 · changed over the centuries 数百年间的变化 · relate the changes to external developments 把变化与外部发展联系起来 · the source of funding 资金来源 · a short paper 一篇短论文 · go into ... in any detail 详细探讨 · brainstorm 头脑风暴

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Stage 1
> **特鲁迪:** 好,斯图尔特。我们得开始规划我们那篇关于公共图书馆的论文了。你想好切入点了吗?
> **斯图尔特:** 嗯,我们能研究的东西太多了。比如图书馆几个世纪以来是怎么变化的,或者不同国家是怎么组织图书馆的。特鲁迪,你怎么看?
> **特鲁迪:** 也许我们该把重点放在本国,并试着把图书馆的变化与外部发展联系起来——比如,能识字的人比一个世纪前多得多,而且本地居民可能讲很多种不同的语言。
> **斯图尔特:** 我们也可以写点关于资金来源变化的内容。
> **特鲁迪:** 可以,但别忘了我们只需要写一篇短论文,所以最好不要太详细地探讨资金问题。
> **斯图尔特:** 对。那我们先头脑风暴几个想法,开个头怎么样?

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Stage 1
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **an angle** (n.) = a particular way of approaching a topic 切入点、角度。→ *Have you thought of an angle?* 你想好从哪个角度写了吗?
> - **changed over the centuries** = 几个世纪以来发生了变化。(*over the centuries* = 数百年间)
> - **relate ... to external developments** = to connect sth to outside changes 把……与外部发展联系起来。(*relate A to B* = 把 A 与 B 关联;*external* = 外部的)
> - **the source of funding** = where the money comes from 资金来源。(*funding* = 经费、拨款)
> - **a short paper** = 一篇篇幅不长的论文。
> - **go into ... in any detail** = to examine sth thoroughly 详细探讨、深入谈。→ 否定式 *don't go into funding in detail* = 不要细谈资金。
> - **brainstorm** (v.) = to quickly produce many ideas together 头脑风暴、集思广益。
>
> **⭐ Scope decisions 范围决定(Part 3 常考):** they will → **focus on this country**, **link libraries to external developments** (literacy, languages). They will **NOT** → cover history across centuries / other countries, and **not** detail **funding** (only a short paper). 他们决定:聚焦本国、把图书馆与外部发展挂钩;**不**写跨世纪历史或他国,也**不**细谈资金。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** The pair settle the paper's scope: focus on one country and link library change to external social developments, keeping funding brief. 两人确定论文范围:聚焦一国、将图书馆变迁与外部社会发展相联系,资金部分从简。

## Stage 2 — The impact of new technology 新技术的影响

**TRUDIE:** OK. We obviously need to look at ==the impact of new technology==, particularly the internet. Now that lots of books have been ==digitalised==, people can ==access== them from their own computers at home.

**STEWART:** And if everyone did that, libraries would be ==obsolete==.

**TRUDIE:** Yes.

**STEWART:** But the digitalised books that are available online for free are mostly ==out of copyright==, aren't they? And ==copyright== in this country lasts for 70 years after the author dies. So you won't find ==the latest best-seller== or up-to-date information.

**TRUDIE:** That's an important point. Anyway, I find it ==hard to concentrate== when I'm reading a long text ==on a screen==. I'd much rather read ==a physical book==. And it takes longer to read on a screen.

**STEWART:** Oh, I prefer it. I suppose it's just ==a personal preference==.

> 🔑 the impact of new technology 新技术的影响 · digitalised 已数字化的 · access 获取/访问 · obsolete 过时的、被淘汰的 · out of copyright 版权已过期 · copyright 版权 · the latest best-seller 最新畅销书 · hard to concentrate 难以专注 · on a screen 在屏幕上 · a physical book 纸质书 · a personal preference 个人偏好

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Stage 2
> **特鲁迪:** 好。我们显然需要看看新技术的影响,尤其是互联网。既然大量图书已经数字化,人们就能在家用自己的电脑获取它们。
> **斯图尔特:** 如果人人都这么做,图书馆就会被淘汰了。
> **特鲁迪:** 是的。
> **斯图尔特:** 可是网上免费提供的那些数字化图书,大多是版权已经过期的,对吧?而本国的版权在作者去世后还要持续70年。所以你找不到最新的畅销书,也找不到最新的信息。
> **特鲁迪:** 这是很重要的一点。不管怎样,读屏幕上的长篇文字时,我觉得很难集中注意力。我更愿意读纸质书。而且在屏幕上读要花更长时间。
> **斯图尔特:** 哦,我倒更喜欢屏幕。我想这只是个人偏好吧。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Stage 2
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **the impact of new technology** = 新技术的影响。(*impact on* = 对……的影响)
> - **digitalised** (adj./v.) = converted into digital form 数字化的。(= *digitised*)
> - **access** (v.) = to get or reach sth (esp. data) 获取、访问。→ *access them from home* 在家就能获取。
> - **obsolete** (adj.) = no longer used; out of date 过时的、被淘汰的。⭐ 高频词。
> - **out of copyright** = no longer protected by copyright 版权已过期(可免费使用)。
> - **copyright** (n.) = the legal right over a creative work 版权、著作权。
> - **the latest best-seller** = 最新的畅销书。(*best-seller* = 畅销书)
> - **hard to concentrate** = 难以集中注意力。(*concentrate* = 专注)
> - **on a screen** = 在(电子)屏幕上。
> - **a physical book** = a printed, paper book 纸质书、实体书。(*physical* here = 实体的,与电子相对)
> - **a personal preference** = an individual choice/taste 个人偏好。
>
> **⭐ Screen vs paper 屏幕 vs 纸质(观点分歧):** **Trudie** = prefers a *physical book*, finds screens *hard to concentrate* on and slower. **Stewart** = *prefers* the screen. → They agree it's just *personal preference*. **特鲁迪**偏爱纸质书、觉得屏幕难专注又慢;**斯图尔特**更爱屏幕——两人都认为这只是个人偏好。
>
> **⭐ Copyright point 版权要点:** free online books are mostly *out of copyright*; copyright lasts **70 years after the author dies**, so the newest books/info aren't free online — libraries are still needed. 网上免费书多是版权过期的;版权在作者去世后持续**70年**,所以最新书和信息网上没有——图书馆仍不可或缺。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** Technology (digitalised, freely-accessible books) could make libraries *obsolete*, but copyright keeps new books off free sites; the two also differ on reading on screen vs paper. 数字化、可免费获取的图书或使图书馆被淘汰,但版权使新书无法免费在线获取;两人在"屏幕 vs 纸质"上看法不同。

## Stage 3 — The future of libraries 图书馆的未来

**TRUDIE:** Mm. I expect that libraries will ==go on evolving== in the next few years. Some have already become centres where ==community activities== take place, like local clubs meeting there. I think that'll become even more common.

**STEWART:** I'd like to think so, and that they'll still be ==serving their traditional function==, but I'm not so sure. There are ==financial implications==, after all. What I'm afraid will happen is that books and magazines will all disappear, and there'll just be ==rows and rows of computers==. They won't look anything like the libraries we're used to.

**TRUDIE:** Well, we'll see.

> 🔑 go on evolving 继续演变 · community activities 社区活动 · serving their traditional function 发挥传统功能 · financial implications 财务影响/资金上的顾虑 · rows and rows of computers 一排排的电脑

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Stage 3
> **特鲁迪:** 嗯。我预计未来几年图书馆会继续演变。有些已经变成了举办社区活动的中心,比如本地俱乐部在那里聚会。我觉得这会变得更加普遍。
> **斯图尔特:** 我也希望如此,并且希望它们仍能发挥传统功能,但我不太确定。毕竟还有资金方面的问题。我担心会发生的是:书和杂志全都消失,只剩下一排排的电脑。那样它们就一点也不像我们熟悉的图书馆了。
> **特鲁迪:** 嗯,我们走着瞧吧。

> [!tip] 📝 Notes — Stage 3
> **Vocabulary & phrases**
> - **go on evolving** = to keep changing/developing 继续演变、不断发展。(*go on doing* = 继续做;*evolve* = 演化)
> - **community activities** = events for local people 社区活动。→ 图书馆成为 *centres where community activities take place*。
> - **serving their traditional function** = doing the job they have always done 发挥其传统功能。(*serve a function* = 起……作用)
> - **financial implications** = the money-related consequences 财务上的影响/后果。(*implications* = 潜在影响)
> - **rows and rows of computers** = 一排又一排的电脑(强调数量多)。
>
> **⭐ Optimist vs pessimist 乐观 vs 悲观(观点分歧):** **Trudie** = optimistic — libraries will *evolve* into *community* centres. **Stewart** = worried — because of *financial implications*, books/magazines may vanish, leaving only *rows of computers*. **特鲁迪**乐观:图书馆会演变为社区中心;**斯图尔特**担忧:因资金问题,书刊或消失,只剩成排电脑。
>
> **Main idea 主旨:** On the future, Trudie foresees libraries evolving into community hubs; Stewart fears money pressures will strip them down to rows of computers. 关于未来,特鲁迪预见图书馆演变为社区中心,斯图尔特则担心资金压力会使其只剩成排电脑。

## Stage 4 — An in-depth study of the local library 对本地图书馆的深入调研

**TRUDIE:** I've just had an idea. Why don't we make an ==in-depth study== of our local public library as background to our paper?

**STEWART:** Yes, that'd be interesting, and ==raise all sorts of issues==. Let's make a list of possible things we could ask about, then work out some sort of structure. For instance, um, we could interview some of the staff, and find out whether the library has ==its own budget==, or if that's controlled by ==the local council==.

**TRUDIE:** And what their ==policies== are. I know they don't allow food, but I'd love to find out what types of noise they ==ban== — there always seems to be a lot of talking, but never music. I don't know if that's a policy or it just happens.

**STEWART:** Ah, I've often wondered. Then there are things like how the library is affected by ==employment laws==. I suppose there are rules about working hours, ==facilities for staff==, and so on.

**TRUDIE:** Right. Then there are other issues relating to ==the design of the building== and how customers use it. Like what measures does the library take to ==ensure their safety==? They'd need ==floor coverings that aren't slippery==, and ==emergency exits==, for instance. Oh, and another thing — there's the question of the kind of ==insurance== the library needs to have, in case anyone gets injured.

**STEWART:** Yes, that's something else to find out. You know something I've often wondered? Well, you know they've got an ==archive== of local newspapers going back years? Well, next to it they've got the diary of a well-known politician from the late 19th century. I wonder why it's there.

**TRUDIE:** No idea. Let's add it to our list. Oh, I've just thought — you know people might ask in the library about local organisations, like sports clubs? Well, I wonder if they keep a ==database==, or whether they just look online.

**STEWART:** Right. I quite fancy finding out what the differences are between a library that's ==open to the public== and one that's part of a museum, for example — they must be very different.

**TRUDIE:** Mmm. Then something else I'd like to know is …

> 🔑 in-depth study 深入研究 · raise all sorts of issues 引出各种问题 · its own budget 自己的预算 · the local council 地方议会 · policies 政策 · ban 禁止 · employment laws 劳动法 · facilities for staff 员工设施 · the design of the building 建筑设计 · ensure their safety 确保安全 · floor coverings that aren't slippery 防滑地面 · emergency exits 紧急出口 · insurance 保险 · archive 档案 · database 数据库 · open to the public 向公众开放

> [!quote] 🇨🇳 中文翻译 — Stage 4
> **特鲁迪:** 我刚想到一个主意。我们何不对本地公共图书馆做一次深入调研,作为论文的背景?
> **斯图尔特:** 好,那会很有意思,还能引出各种各样的问题。我们先列一张可以询问的事项清单,然后理出某种结构。比如,我们可以采访一些工作人员,弄清楚图书馆是有自己的预算,还是由地方议会控制。
> **特鲁迪:** 还有他们的各项政策。我知道他们不许带食物,但我很想知道他们禁止哪些类型的噪音——那里似乎总有很多说话声,却从没有音乐。我不知道那是一项政策,还是自然而然形成的。
> **斯图尔特:** 啊,我也常常纳闷这个。还有像图书馆如何受劳动法影响之类的问题。我想应该有关于工作时间、员工设施等等的规定。
> **特鲁迪:** 对。还有一些与建筑设计以及顾客如何使用图书馆相关的问题。比如,图书馆采取哪些措施来确保他们的安全?比如说,他们需要防滑的地面材料和紧急出口。哦,还有一件事——还有图书馆需要投保哪种保险的问题,以防有人受伤。
> **斯图尔特:** 对,那也是要查的一件事。你知道我常纳闷的一件事吗?你知道他们有一份追溯多年的本地报纸档案吧?就在它旁边,他们还放着一位19世纪末知名政治家的日记。我很好奇它为什么会在那儿。
> **特鲁迪:** 不知道。把它加到我们的清单里吧。哦,我刚想到——你知道人们可能会在图书馆询问本地组织的情况,比如体育俱乐部?我想知道他们是保有一个数据库,还是只是上网查。
> **斯图尔特:** 对。我还挺想弄清楚,一座向公众开放的图书馆,和一座属于博物馆一部分的图书馆之间有什么区别——它们一定很不一样。
> **特鲁迪:** 嗯。然后我还想知道的另一件事是……
