
Questions and band 8 answers based on Simon's official Part 1 topic list. Keep answers short (2–3 sentences): a direct answer + a quick reason or example, said naturally with contractions.

**Note on "(negative answers)":** Simon deliberately includes these to show you *don't* have to be positive about everything. A confident, honest "no" with a reason scores just as well as a "yes."

## Quick tips
- **Answer, then extend** — say your answer, then a quick *because / but / so*.
- **It's fine to be negative** — a confident "not really, to be honest..." with a reason scores well.
- **Use contractions and fillers** — "I'd say", "to be honest", "actually" sound natural and buy thinking time.
- **Don't memorise word-for-word** — learn the pattern and the vocabulary, not the script.
