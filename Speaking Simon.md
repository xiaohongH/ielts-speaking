# IELTS Speaking — Simon's Method

## Part 1

**Format**
- 4–5 minutes
- 10–11 questions
- Covers a few topic areas

**Remember**
1. Try to show fluency.
2. Don't stop and think.
3. Don't worry about vocabulary or grammar.

It's an easy warm-up.

**Finishing an answer**
- Stop with confidence — don't keep talking.
- Smile at the examiner.

**Topics often seem childish** — so answer simply and naturally:
- *What's your favourite colour?* → "My favourite colour is blue, because it's the colour of the sky on a nice day."

**A good way to practise** — "Do you like...?" questions:
- games, walking, gardens, flowers, dancing

**Structure of Part 1**
1. Introduction topics (2 or 3):
   - Do you work, or are you a student?
   - Do you enjoy your job / studies?
   - Would you like to get a different job in the future?
2. Four questions on one topic — "Let's talk about sport..."
3. Four questions on a second topic — "Let's talk about colours..."

---

## Part 2

**Format**
- 3–4 minutes in total
- 1 minute to prepare
- Speak for 2 minutes

**6 main topic areas**
- Describe a person, a place, an object, an event, an activity, or your favorites.

**How to prepare**
- Prepare ideas and vocabulary — not grammar or linking.
- Speak naturally and explain in detail.
- Focus on content, not structure.

**"Describe a person" — types to prepare**
- a teacher
- a famous person
- a friend
- a family member
- a child
- someone who helps people
- someone who does something well

---

## Part 3

Give long, detailed answers — this is your final chance to impress the examiner. 
Force yourself to follow these steps, otherwise your answers won't have 'direction'.

**Steps**
1. Answer the question directly.
2. Explain your answer (give reasons).
3. Give an example.
4. Explain the opposite / alternative (what would happen if...).
