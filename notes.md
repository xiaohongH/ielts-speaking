# IELTS Speaking - The Four Gentlemen

## Version 1 (Band 7 - Natural and Clear)

I’d like to talk about a traditional Chinese cultural symbol called the Four Gentlemen, which are the plum blossom, orchid, bamboo, and chrysanthemum. These four plants are very important in Chinese art and literature, because each of them represents a different personality trait.

The plum blossom usually blooms in winter, sometimes even in snow, so it symbolizes courage and hope in difficult times. The orchid often grows in quiet valleys and has a light, elegant fragrance, so it stands for humility and inner beauty. Bamboo grows straight and stays green all year, and it can bend in strong wind without breaking, so it represents honesty, loyalty, and resilience. Finally, the chrysanthemum blooms in late autumn, when many other flowers are fading, so it symbolizes calmness and integrity.

Among them, I especially like chrysanthemum because it reminds me of a simple and peaceful lifestyle. There is also a famous poet, Tao Yuanming, who loved chrysanthemums and chose a quiet countryside life instead of a stressful official career. I think that idea is still meaningful today, because many people are under pressure and want a more balanced life.

Overall, the Four Gentlemen are not just plants. They reflect the values Chinese people have admired for centuries.

### 中文翻译（版本1）

我想谈谈一个中国传统文化符号，叫做“四君子”，也就是梅、兰、竹、菊。这四种植物在中国艺术和文学中非常重要，因为它们各自代表不同的品格。

梅花通常在冬天开放，有时甚至在雪中绽放，因此它象征着在困难时期的勇气和希望。兰花常生长在安静的山谷中，香气清雅，所以它代表谦逊和内在之美。竹子挺拔笔直、四季常青，而且在强风中可以弯曲却不折断，因此它象征诚实、忠诚和韧性。最后，菊花在深秋开放，那时很多花都凋谢了，所以它象征平和与操守。

在这四者中，我尤其喜欢菊花，因为它会让我想到一种简单而宁静的生活方式。还有一位著名诗人陶渊明，他喜爱菊花，并选择了宁静的田园生活，而不是压力很大的仕途。我认为这种理念在今天仍然很有意义，因为很多人都承受着压力，也希望生活更平衡。

总的来说，四君子不仅仅是植物，它们体现了中国人几个世纪以来所推崇的价值观。

## Version 2 (Band 8+ - Advanced Vocabulary)

I’d like to describe a traditional Chinese concept known as the Four Gentlemen, namely the plum blossom, orchid, bamboo, and chrysanthemum. In Chinese culture, these are far more than decorative plants; they serve as moral symbols and embody qualities associated with an ideal character.

To begin with, the plum blossom flowers in the depth of winter, sometimes against snow, so it is widely associated with ==fortitude, optimism, and the courage to endure adversity.== The orchid, by contrast, is ==understated and delicate==. It often grows in secluded places and gives off a subtle fragrance, which is why it symbolizes ==modesty, refinement, and inner nobility rather than outward showiness.==

As for bamboo, it is admired for being upright and evergreen. What is particularly meaningful is that it bends in strong winds without snapping, so it conveys both ==integrity and resilience==. Finally, the chrysanthemum blooms in late autumn, when many other flowers have withered, and therefore represents ==emotional composure, independence, and faithfulness to one’s principles.==

Personally, I’m most drawn to the chrysanthemum because it reminds me of a quieter and more self-directed way of life. It is often linked to the poet Tao Yuanming, who turned away from official ambition and embraced a simple rural life. I find that perspective extremely relevant today, since many people are rethinking success and prioritizing mental well-being over social status.

Overall, the Four Gentlemen capture a core aspect of Chinese aesthetics: the belief that nature can mirror moral character and offer guidance for how to live.

### 中文翻译（版本2）

我想描述一个中国传统概念，叫做“四君子”，即梅花、兰花、竹子和菊花。在中国文化中，它们远不只是观赏植物；它们承载着道德象征，体现了理想人格所具备的品质。

首先，梅花在寒冬时节开放，有时傲雪而开，因此常被赋予==坚韧、乐观以及直面逆境的勇气==。相比之下，兰花更含蓄而雅致。它常生于幽僻之处，散发淡雅清香，所以它象征==谦逊、修养和内在高贵，而非外在张扬。==

至于竹子，它因挺拔和常青而备受推崇。尤其有意义的是，它在强风中会弯曲却不会折断，因此同时传达了正直与韧性。最后，菊花在晚秋盛放，当许多花朵已经凋零，因此它象征==情绪上的从容、独立精神以及对自身原则的坚守。==

就我个人而言，我最偏爱菊花，因为它让我想到一种更安静、更自主的生活方式。它常与诗人陶渊明联系在一起；他放弃仕途追求，选择了朴素的田园生活。我认为这种价值观在当下仍然非常贴切，因为许多人正在重新思考“成功”的定义，并把心理健康置于社会地位之上。

总而言之，四君子体现了中国美学中的核心理念：自然可以映照人的品格，并为人们提供生活方式上的启发。
