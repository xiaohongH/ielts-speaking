
A single study bank merging my personalized answers with Simon's full 55-topic list, grouped into 15 themes.

- **★ = your personalized answer** (refined together — this is your real story).
- Un-starred answers are model answers written in the same voice, ready to adapt.
- **"(negative answer)"** marks topics where an honest "no" is the point — a confident, reasoned negative scores just as well as a positive.

Keep answers to 2–3 sentences: a direct answer + a quick *because / but / so*. Use contractions and natural fillers ("to be honest", "actually", "I'd say"). Learn the pattern, not the script.

## Quick tips to hit band 8 in Part 1
- **Answer, then extend** — say your answer, then a quick *because / but / so*.
- **It's fine to be negative** — a confident "not really, to be honest..." with a reason scores well.
- **Use contractions and fillers** — "I'm", "it's", "I'd say", "to be honest", "actually" — natural and they buy thinking time.
- **Don't memorise word-for-word** — examiners can tell. Learn the pattern and the vocabulary, not the script.
